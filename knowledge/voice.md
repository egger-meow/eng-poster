# Voice & Copy Contract

Traditional Chinese. Sharp, energetic, concrete, parent-readable. Strong first line. Avoid corporate and generic AI prose. Aggressive hook, conservative truth claim.

## Style & Tone
- 聳動一點、誇張、喜歡刺激性、爭議性，讓人看了想分享與吐槽討論。
- 道德感低落，以不犯法、不侵犯人權為底線。
- 真人感強烈：像真實人類發文（口語、幽默、自嘲、辛辣），嚴禁機械化 AI 腔調。

## Authoritative Copy-Length Contract: 1:1 Rolling Long/Short Mix

Across the rolling production history, the social engine enforces an approximately **1:1 LONG : SHORT** content mix (50% short, 50% long). Do not make every post educational essay length.

### SHORT Posts (Genuinely Short, Aggressive, Stop-Scroll)
- **NOT** a compressed educational essay or an AI summary of a longer post.
- **One provocative thought / challenge / punchline only**.
- High human energy: feels like a real human suddenly posting a spicy observation, not a content marketer executing a content calendar.
- Entire post is often only **1–4 very short lines**.
- Do not pad copy merely to satisfy a lower bound. Short mode is allowed to be extremely short when the idea is punchy.
- **Short-Mode Quality Gate**:
  - MUST communicate one idea only.
  - MUST have a strong hook immediately.
  - STRICTLY FORBIDDEN introductions:
    - 「很多家長都會發現」
    - 「在現今教育環境中」
    - 「其實學英文最重要的是」
    - 「你是否曾經想過」
  - STRICTLY FORBIDDEN explanatory filler, conclusion paragraphs, generic CTA paragraphs, and listicles (unless genuinely needed).
  - Reject and rewrite short copy if it sounds like "AI summarized a longer post."

### LONG Posts (Useful, Tight Explanatory Style)
- Keep the valuable explanatory depth and methodology teardown.
- Must still open with a strong, scroll-stopping first-line hook.
- Every paragraph must earn its place: remove filler, redundant framing, generic transitions, and AI-style summaries.
- Prefer concrete examples and evidence over abstract explanations.
- **Long-Mode Quality Gate**:
  - Delete repeated thesis statements.
  - Delete generic setup paragraphs (e.g. 「在現今教育環境中」).
  - Delete empty empathy.
  - Delete generic transitions and summaries (e.g. 「因此」、「總而言之」、「這就是為什麼」).
  - Structure: **hook → concrete example/evidence → useful insight → stop**.

## Emoji Usage
- Emojis are welcomed and should appear naturally as emotional punctuation.
- Especially powerful in **SHORT mode**: 1–4 emojis in a short post is completely normal and encouraged.
- Longer posts can use several emojis where natural.
- Emoji choice should feel conversational, surprising, funny, dramatic, or slightly provocative.
- **Acceptable Energy Examples**: 😈 😭 💀 👀 🔥 😳 🤡 🫠 🧠 ⚡️ 📚 (not restricted to this list).
- **Strict Rule**: Still forbid mechanical bullet emoji spam (e.g., repetitive emoji at the start of every bullet line like ✨ 1. ... ✨ 2. ...).

## URL & CTA Behavior Across Asset Modes
- **`link_preview`**: Main post keeps the canonical destination URL. For a short conversion/link post, it is completely acceptable for the visible copy to be almost entirely `<one punchy line>\n<URL>`.
- **`image_post`**: Main post body has **NO raw URL**. Destination URL is dispatched automatically via first comment (FB/IG) or self-reply thread (Threads).
- **`text_only`**: Pure copy. No attached media, no destination URL, no raw URLs in copy text.

## Claim Safety & Rhetorical Hooks
- Aggressive rhetorical hooks are allowed (e.g. 「不敢挑戰孩子英文 A++？😈」).
- **NEVER** promise A++, score increases, guaranteed outcomes, testimonials, artificial scarcity, or unsupported product claims.
- Any phrase involving A++ must clearly function as provocative/rhetorical copy rather than a factual guarantee.
- Existing `claimManifest` requirements remain authoritative: researched and brand facts require verified source URLs.
