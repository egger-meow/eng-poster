# Paper English Automated Content Playbook

## Mission

Create high-attention Traditional Chinese organic social content that makes Taiwan parents/students curious enough to remember Paper English, inspect real materials, or visit the site.

## Voice

- sharp;
- energetic;
- concrete;
- parent-readable;
- not academic-journal prose;
- not corporate press-release prose;
- not generic "AI education revolution" fluff.

## Hook rule

First line must earn the second line.

Prefer:

- pain;
- contradiction;
- surprising observation;
- recognizable parent/student scene;
- specific question;
- strong claim that is supportable.

Avoid empty hooks such as:

- 「你知道嗎？」
- 「在這個快速變化的時代」
- 「英文學習非常重要」
- 「AI 正在改變教育」

unless the rest is unusually specific and the opening has been rewritten.

## Aggressive but factual

Desired:

> 孩子不是討厭英文，他可能只是受夠了無聊教材。

Not allowed without evidence:

> 90% 的孩子都因為學校教材太無聊而英文不好。

Desired:

> 背了 30 個單字，隔天忘掉一大半。再多背 30 個，真的會比較好嗎？

Not allowed:

> 傳統單字書根本沒用。

## Paper English positioning

Keep returning to a small number of memorable ideas instead of feature soup:

1. interest-first English content;
2. real curriculum/exam alignment rather than random entertainment;
3. personalized material that remains educationally purposeful;
4. tangible material examples parents can inspect.

Exact claims must come from `knowledge/product.md` and `knowledge/claims.md`.

## Suggested rolling mix

- 25% pain/opinion (`pain_point`)
- 20% parent-perspective creative story (`parent_story`)
- 20% useful learning insight (`educational_value`)
- 15% product/material proof (`product_proof`)
- 10% timely topic (`timely_topic`)
- 10% direct conversion (`conversion_offer`)

## Parent-Perspective Creative Story (`parent_story` Archetype)

First-person, authentic Taiwanese parent narrative (grades 5–8 / 國七國八家長). The goal is deep emotional resonance followed by a sharp, delightful, or self-deprecating pivot to Paper English.

Key tropes & tension points:
- Domestic friction: homework dragging until 10pm, private school pressure, fighting over highlighters/study notes.
- The 3C vs paper dilemma: kids glued to phones, but willing to read physical print when the topic is electric.
- The sudden realization: kids don't hate reading English, they hate generic, dead-eyed workbooks.
- The pivot: "好啦我不演了" / "剛剛去翻他書包破案了" / "後來我氣到印了一張丟在他桌上", linking directly to `paperbond.jjmowlab.com` with current offer claims.

## CTA intensity

Do not make every post "buy now".

Suggested:

- 50% no hard CTA;
- 30% soft CTA: 看看教材、看看怎麼做、留言/分享觀點;
- 20% direct CTA: free example/current offer/site visit.

## Threads

Default:

- 2/day;
- opinionated;
- concise;
- can be text-only;
- strong experiments are welcome;
- avoid posting the same idea twice in one week unless a genuinely new angle exists.

## Facebook

Default:

- 4/week;
- explain enough that a parent can understand the educational point;
- product screenshots/material excerpts work well;
- links are acceptable when relevant.

## Instagram

Default:

- 3/week;
- visual must carry an idea;
- image/caption should complement each other;
- prefer one clean message over dense poster text;
- use deterministic typography for Traditional Chinese overlays.

## Timely-topic transformation

Do not chase a trend merely because it is trending.

A timely topic is valid when at least one is true:

- students genuinely care about it;
- parents are discussing it;
- it can teach vocabulary/reading/context naturally;
- it demonstrates interest-based material generation;
- it creates a useful educational discussion.

## Diversity contracts

Rolling 14 days should avoid:

- same opening structure > 3 times;
- same primary archetype > 3 consecutive posts on one platform;
- exact same CTA > 3 times;
- same media asset within 30 days;
- same visual concept within 7 days where alternatives exist;
- repeated "孩子不是 X，是 Y" formula more than twice per 14 days;
- repetitive emoji patterns.

## Claim contract

Every factual sentence is one of:

- brand fact: supported by checked-in Paper English knowledge;
- researched fact: linked to stored current research source;
- opinion: clearly framed as opinion/interpretation;
- rhetorical copy: non-factual.

If a sentence cannot be classified, rewrite/remove it.

## Conversion links

All website links use UTM attribution. Do not paste naked canonical links if the post is published by this engine.
